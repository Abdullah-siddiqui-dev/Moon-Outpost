🌙 Moon Outpost

Your mission: survive 10 days

You run a base on the Moon. Every day you get some power and must split it:

💨 Air machine makes oxygen to breathe.
🛡️ Shield blocks space radiation.
🔬 Science earns points, but gives no air or shield.

Power is limited, so more for one job means less for another. Sometimes the Sun sends a solar storm. You get a warning first!

Real NASA science

The solar storms are real events from the past. They are saved inside the game (archived), not loaded live. Their strength is scaled down for kids.

Data: NASA DONKI (solar storms), OSDR RadLab (radiation on the Space Station), NASA OSDR (astronaut health). "Did you know?" numbers are rounded from public NASA info.
